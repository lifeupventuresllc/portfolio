Chicken Nugget Hash Brown Bake
1 serving per bowl
354 cal 46g P 7g F 23g C

Ingredients (per bowl)

  • ½ cup shredded hash browns
  • 1 whole egg
  • ¼ cup egg whites
  • 4 oz lightly breaded chicken nuggets
  • 1 oz fat-free cheese
  • Salt + pepper
  • 1–2 tbsp pico de gallo

Directions

  1. Mix all base ingredients in a 13 oz oven-safe container.
  2. Bake at 375°F for 30 min until eggs are cooked through.
  3. Add cheese on top and heat again until melted.
  4. Top with pico and enjoy.
Egg-free version: omit whole egg and egg whites, add 2 oz extra chicken nuggets.

BBQ Chicken Pizza
4 slices
~280 cal ~32g P ~10g F ~22g C

Ingredients

  • 10 oz chicken breast
  • 1 thin pizza crust
  • 2–3 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 2 oz shredded cheese (fat-free or low-fat)
  • 1 oz onion
  • 2 oz jalapeno
  • Italian seasoning, salt, red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Season chicken with Italian, salt, red pepper. Cook 3–5 min per side (160°F+).
  2. Dice chicken.
  3. Spread BBQ sauce on pizza crust.
  4. Add cheese evenly. Top with chicken, onion, jalapeno.
  5. Bake 425°F for 10 min until crispy and melted.
  6. Cut into 4 slices.

Chicken Pineapple Fried Rice
5 servings
613 cal 66g P 12g F 56g C

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken breast
  • 2⅔ cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1½ cups pineapple
  • 6 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1½ cups diced bell peppers
  • ¾ cup diced onion
  • 3 eggs
  • 6 tbsp sugar-free ketchup

Directions

  1. Dice chicken, season, cook in wok or large skillet.
  2. Scramble 3 eggs in same pan, break into pieces.
  3. Add diced peppers and onion, sauté 3–4 min.
  4. Add cooked rice, pineapple, mixed veggies. Stir-fry together.
  5. Add soy sauce and sugar-free ketchup. Toss to combine.
  6. Distribute into 5 containers.
Recipe 3 Also in Dinner
Chicken Teriyaki Rice Bowl
4 servings
~380 cal 36g P 5g F 42g C

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast
  • 18 oz cooked jasmine rice
  • 14 oz broccoli florets
  • 4 tbsp low-sodium teriyaki sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¾ tsp ground ginger
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • Optional: sesame seeds, green onion

Directions

  1. Dice chicken. Season with garlic, ginger, pepper.
  2. Cook chicken in skillet with sesame oil, 5–6 min. Set aside.
  3. Steam or roast broccoli until tender-crisp (~5 min).
  4. Cook rice per package directions.
  5. Toss chicken with teriyaki sauce.
  6. Portion into 4 containers: rice → broccoli → chicken.
Recipe 5 Also in Dinner
Chicken & Fries with Cane’s Sauce
4 servings
603 cal 57g P 18g F 47g C

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp hot honey
  • 1 bag Alexia sweet potato fries (566g)
  • Salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, sea salt
Cane’s Sauce
  • ½ cup fat-free Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup light mayo
  • 2 tbsp sugar-free ketchup
  • 1 tsp honey mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

Directions

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, sea salt.
  2. Cook chicken (grill, skillet, or bake at 400°F until 165°F internal).
  3. Air fry sweet potato fries per package directions.
  4. Mix all Cane’s sauce ingredients together.
  5. Slice chicken, toss with hot honey.
  6. Divide everything into 4 meal preps.
