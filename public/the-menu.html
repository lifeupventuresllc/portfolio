Chicken Nugget Hash Brown Bake
1 serving per bowl
354 cal 46g P 7g F 23g C

Ingredients (per bowl)

  • ½ cup shredded hash browns
  • 1 whole egg
  • ¼ cup egg whites
  • 4 oz lightly breaded chicken nuggets
  • 1 oz fat-free cheese
  • Salt + pepper
  • 1–2 tbsp pico de gallo

Directions

  1. Mix all base ingredients in a 13 oz oven-safe container.
  2. Bake at 375°F for 30 min until eggs are cooked through.
  3. Add cheese on top and heat again until melted.
  4. Top with pico and enjoy.
Egg-free version: omit whole egg and egg whites, add 2 oz extra chicken nuggets.

Recipe 8
Beef & Egg Breakfast Power Bowl
10 servings
552 cal 51g P 20g F 40g C

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs 96/4 ground beef
  • 20 slices beef bacon (45-cal kind)
  • 20 eggs
  • 4 lbs (64 oz) frozen south style potatoes
  • 1½ tsp paprika
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1¼ tsp onion powder
  • 1¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¾ tsp all-purpose seasoning (for eggs)

Directions

  1. Season beef with paprika, garlic, onion, pepper, salt. Brown in large skillet. Drain.
  2. Chop bacon into small pieces. Cook until crispy.
  3. Scramble eggs with all-purpose seasoning.
  4. Spread potatoes on sheet pan. Spray with oil. Bake 400°F for 20 min. Broil 500°F for 10 min.
  5. Combine all ingredients. Mix evenly.
  6. Weigh total. Divide by 10. Portion into 10 containers.
Sauces optional — not included in macros.

Recipe 15
Southwest Bacon Breakfast Burritos
7 burritos
527 cal 37g P 20g F 44g C

Ingredients

  • 7 burrito-sized tortillas
  • 7 whole eggs
  • 1 lb 96/4 ground beef
  • 10 slices center-cut bacon
  • 4 oz reduced-fat Mexican-style cheese
  • 1 bag (~10 oz) southwestern burrito bowl mix (Good & Gather, Target frozen)

Directions

  1. Cook ground beef in one pan, bacon in the other.
  2. After beef is 50% cooked, add southwestern veggie mix.
  3. Chop bacon into pieces. Add to beef mixture.
  4. Scramble eggs separately. Fold into the mixture.
  5. Add cheese. Stir until melted and combined.
  6. Distribute evenly into 7 tortillas. Roll up tightly.
  7. Wrap in foil. Reheat in oven or air fryer.
Egg-free version: omit eggs, add 4 oz extra ground beef. ~490 cal · 38g P per burrito.

Turkey Stuffed Bagel Meal Prep
4 servings
~450 cal ~48g P ~14g F ~30g C

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 97/3 ground turkey
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 small white onion, diced
  • 1 small green pepper, diced
  • 1–2 cups crimini mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 4 Plain Dave’s Killer Bagels

Recipe 20 New
Protein Banana Bread
~8 slices
~180 cal ~12g P ~4g F ~25g C

Ingredients

  • Ripe bananas
  • Egg whites
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt
  • Vanilla protein powder
  • Oat flour
  • Baking powder
  • Baking soda
  • Monkfruit sweetener
  • Salt
  • Mini chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Mash bananas. Mix in egg whites and Greek yogurt until smooth.
  2. Stir in vanilla protein powder.
  3. Add dry ingredients: oat flour, baking powder, baking soda, sweetener, salt. Don’t over mix — just combine.
  4. Pour into loaf pan (optional: line with parchment).
  5. Throw chocolate chips on top (or mix in batter).
  6. Bake at 350°F for 40–45 min. Ready when toothpick comes out clean, top is golden, center is set.
  7. Cool 15–20 min before slicing.

Recipe 21 New
Protein Pancake Bake
12 servings
289 cal 25g P 10g F 25g C

Ingredients

  • 4 cups Kodiak Buttermilk Pancake Mix
  • 3 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2–3 tbsp calorie-free sweetener
  • 1 lb turkey breakfast sausage (cooked)
  • 8 slices turkey bacon (cooked & chopped)
  • 1½ cups lite shredded cheese
  • Lite syrup (drizzle)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook sausage and bacon. Set aside.
  3. Mix pancake mix, almond milk, eggs, and sweetener until smooth.
  4. Fold in sausage, bacon, and cheese.
  5. Pour into a greased 9×13 baking dish.
  6. Bake 30–35 minutes, until set and golden.
  7. Cool, slice into 12 pieces, store in fridge. Reheat, add lite syrup, and go.

Recipe 22 New
Sourdough Pizza Toast
1 serving (2 slices)
~540 cal 43g P 21g F 44g C

Ingredients

  • 2 large slices sourdough bread (100g)
  • ¼ cup marinara or pizza sauce (60g)
  • ½ cup low-fat cottage cheese (70g)
  • ¼ cup low-fat shredded mozzarella cheese (28g)
  • 14 slices turkey pepperoni (30g)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning (2g)
  • ½ tsp garlic powder (1g)
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp hot honey (15ml)
  • Fresh parsley or basil for garnish

Directions

  1. Toast sourdough slices in toaster or air fryer for 2–3 minutes until slightly firm.
  2. Blend the cottage cheese in a food processor until smooth.
  3. Spread marinara sauce evenly over toasted slices. Add a layer of cottage cheese on top.
  4. Distribute shredded mozzarella evenly. Top each slice with turkey pepperoni, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder.
  5. Air fry at 375°F for 5–7 minutes, or bake in oven at 400°F for 8–10 minutes, until cheese is melted and pepperoni is slightly crispy.
  6. Remove. Drizzle hot honey over top, sprinkle with red pepper flakes, garnish with parsley or basil.

Recipe 23 New
Creamy Lemon Chicken Orzo
4 servings
540 cal 43g P 21g F 44g C

Ingredients

For the Chicken
  • 1.75 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs (800g)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
For the Orzo
  • 1½ cups dry orzo pasta (300g)
  • 1 small yellow onion (finely diced)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth (720ml)
  • ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt or light cream (120g)
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Juice of 1 large lemon
Add-ins
  • 3 cups fresh spinach (90g)
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese (30g)

Directions

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, oregano.
  2. Sear chicken in olive oil over medium-high heat, 4–5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In same pan, add onion and cook 2–3 minutes until softened, then add garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Toast dry orzo in the pan for 1 minute to lightly brown.
  5. Pour in chicken broth, bring to a gentle simmer, and cook 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until orzo is tender and creamy.
  6. Lower heat and stir in Greek yogurt, lemon zest, lemon juice, and Parmesan until smooth.
  7. Add spinach. Fold in and let it wilt into the orzo.
  8. Nestle chicken back into the pan and simmer 2–3 minutes to warm through and absorb flavor.

Protein Mac & Cheese Bake
Turkey & Marinara  |  ~6 servings
~480 cal ~45g P ~15g F ~35g C

Meat & Veggies

  • 1 lb 93/7 ground turkey
  • 1 tbsp olive or avocado oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 (8 oz) carton baby bella mushrooms, sliced
  • Onion powder, garlic powder, black pepper, paprika
Meat Sauce
  • 1 (16 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • Chopped sun-dried tomatoes (not in oil)
  • 2 cups Rao’s Marinara
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped
Mac & Cheese Layer
  • 1½ cups protein pasta (uncooked)
  • 1 (16 oz) low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1½ cups low-fat shredded cheese
  • 1 stick lite string cheese, torn
  • ½ cup almond milk
  • 5–7 slices lite mozzarella (topping)
  • ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ¼ tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp arrowroot powder + 2 tbsp water (slurry)

Directions

  1. Cook protein pasta. Drain.
  2. Brown turkey with oil, garlic, and mushrooms. Season generously.
  3. Make meat sauce: add diced tomatoes, sun-dried tomatoes, Rao’s, and basil. Simmer 10 min.
  4. Make cheese sauce: blend cottage cheese, shredded cheese, almond milk, seasonings, arrowroot slurry. Stir into pasta.
  5. Layer in baking dish: pasta cheese mixture → meat sauce → mozzarella & string cheese.
  6. Bake at 375°F for 25–30 min. Broil 2 min for crispy top.

Recipe 24 New
Fully Loaded Fried Cabbage
~5 servings
~320 cal 33g P 10g F 15g C

Ingredients

  • 1 lb raw shrimp (peeled & deveined)
  • 1 lb chicken sausage (5 links, cooked & sliced)
  • 5 slices nitrate-free turkey bacon (chopped)
  • 1 head green cabbage (chopped)
  • 2 red bell peppers (chopped)
  • 1 red onion (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 2–3 tbsp avocado or olive oil
  • Garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, Cajun seasoning to taste

Directions

  1. Prep everything — chop peppers, onion, cabbage, bacon, slice sausage.
  2. Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in large pot over medium heat. Add turkey bacon and sausage. Cook until browned. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add shrimp to same pot. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, pepper, Cajun. Cook 1–2 min per side until just pink. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add more oil if needed. Add onion until translucent. Add bell peppers, minced garlic, pepper, Cajun. Stir well.
  5. Add cabbage in batches so it wilts evenly. Do not cover. Season as it cooks.
  6. Return turkey bacon, sausage, and shrimp to pot. Toss until combined and heated through.
  7. Serve hot.

  1. Mix dry ingredients. Add water gradually until soft dough forms.
